# Working paper

[Read the manuscript](manuscript.md). This is a working paper for methodological feedback, not a
peer-reviewed publication. The abstract and empirical tables distinguish reported historical
estimates from publicly runnable verification.

Feedback is especially useful on identification from market mids, queue assumptions, interpretation
of the favorable-cost rejection, and what additional evidence would improve independent reproduction.

The D12 table is transcribed from `evidence/d12_public_verdict.json`; original estimation is not rerun
by building this document. The pricing summaries are author-reported from the original research
record and are explicitly labeled as such. The synthetic walkthrough is not a research figure.
